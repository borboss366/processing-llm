# Far-side flip: per-side deviation sign in profile transfer

User observation on the measured-rest QA: far-side limbs (lifted leg,
far arm) mirrored backward while near-side limbs track — a per-side
rotation sign missing in the transfer. Also predicted, correctly, that
the existing round-trip check would read 0 because it compared through
the same convention.

## (1) The measurement (root-diag, side-aware check)

The check was rebuilt: expected rendered deviation = sideSign ×
observed deviation, where sideSign is −1 for the side whose rig foot
opposes the profile facing (the human→rig rest mapping for that side is
a reflection = orientation-reversing). Pre-fix, per-bone worst over the
window — the predicted 2× signature, exact:

| bone | err | obs deviation | sideSign |
|---|---|---|---|
| hipL | 95.4° | −47.7° | −1 |
| kneeL | 88.8° | 44.4° | −1 |
| ankleL | 128.6° | 64.3° | −1 |
| shoulderL | 120.1° | −60.1° | −1 |
| elbowL | 37.4° | −18.7° | −1 |
| all R-side bones + chest | 0.0° | — | +1 |

Every flipped-side bone erred at exactly 2× its deviation; every
near-side bone at 0 — the signature of a sign, not noise.

## (2) The fix

`sideSigns(rig, view)` in retarget.mjs: in profile view, the deviation
acc is negated for the side whose rig rest foot points against the
facing (from sidecar geometry, no hardcoded side). Applied ONCE at the
acc level so parent chains of mixed sign stay consistent (torso is
always +1; FK reconstructs acc from theta sums). Frontal capture is
untouched — there the human's own sides already move mirrored and the
identity transfer was correct all along.

Post-fix: every bone 0.0° both sides (side-aware round-trip), clamp
hits still 0. QA re-rendered: both legs lift forward, both arms pump
the same way — the backward-kicking far leg is gone.

## (3) Self-test

Two new permanent checks (13 total, PASS): synthetic profile knee-lift
on EACH side through the full mechanism (calibMasks → measureRest →
retarget with side sign → FK), asserting side-aware round-trip < 1° on
both sides — 0.00° L and R. Pre-fix the far side read ~80° (2× the
40° lift).

Pipeline experiment: nothing staged, no tables rebuilt; USER_GATES
item 3 hold stands. The as-filmed profile transfer is now: measured
rest + heel→toe feet + per-side sign — three user-diagnosed layers,
each with its own regression test.
