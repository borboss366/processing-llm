## What changed

- **Sidecars** (biped-1, biped-2, biped-front): `shoulderL/R` inserted at
  40% along chest→elbow (role `shoulder`), `ankleL/R` at 72% along
  knee→foot (role `ankle`); parents rewired chest→shoulder→elbow→hand
  and pelvis→knee→ankle→foot. Ghost untouched (grep: its "shoulders"
  keep role `knee`; schema-compatible). biped-front is also the audience
  template — the submit service picks it up on next boot; already
  approved audience sidecars keep their old chains and remain loadable
  (missing roles simply mean pre-14 behavior).
- **Engine** (creature.js):
  - GAITS gains `shoulder`/`ankle` role functions in all four gaits; arm
    amplitudes rebalanced (wrist 0.40→0.28, elbow 0.28→0.24, shoulder
    0.16 new) because the FK chain now accumulates three rotations.
  - Walk override generalized: the whole grounded leg is placed off the
    hip→foot segment (knee 50% + forward bias, ankle 80%) so odometry
    and the chain can't half-disagree.
  - Stance lock extended with the **heel pivot**: while a ground tip is
    contact-locked, an authored ankle `rot` swings the ankle ABOUT the
    planted toe (FK's toe-about-ankle inverted on contact) — the tstep
    mechanism. Knee follows the hip↔ankle midpoint.
  - Pins, bones, bone-length measurement, draw radii and splat routing
    all derive from the joints list — extended automatically.
- **Tables** re-authored for the new joints:
  - `armwave`: generated sinusoids, f=2 per 8-beat loop; originates at
    the shoulder (±0.5 rad), elbow opens through 0.715–1.285 rad,
    per-link lag 0.2 beat (0.025 loop) shoulder→elbow→wrist; right arm
    mirrored at lower amplitude, half-loop offset. 32 keys — at 8 keys
    the sub-key lag flattened to zero in keyframe quantization.
  - `tstep`: weight-side heel pivot via ankle rot (±0.35) against honest
    single-foot contacts; kick side carries knee+ankle+toe; arms
    counter-sway via shoulders.
  - `groove`: unchanged character + subtle shoulder counter-sway (±0.1/0.12).
- **MODULE_ABI.md**: role set + chain layout + heel-pivot semantics.
- **fk-check** extended: heel-pivot assert (planted toe ≤ 6 px drift,
  ankle ≥ 4 px swing), shoulder→elbow AND elbow→wrist wave-travel
  asserts via first-harmonic phase projection (argmax on the 1/16 scrub
  grid cannot resolve a 0.025-loop lag; the projection reads −φ₀, so
  outward travel is decreasing phase — calibrated against the authored
  table), webm now cycles all three moves.
