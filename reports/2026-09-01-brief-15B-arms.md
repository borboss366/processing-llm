## Engine fixes found by the harness (each a real bug)

1. **Workbench scrub aliasing**: `manualBase` was pinned at manual entry
   against THAT move's beatsPerLoop; switching to a different-bpl move
   aliased every scrub by base%bpl (measured: scrub 0.02 → loop 0.52).
   Base now re-aligns per active move.
2. **Manual move-switch blend freeze**: switching moves while scrubbed
   started a pose blend that rides the pinned clock — frozen on the old
   pose (same class as the documented hot-swap rule; now guarded, and
   the instant swap declares a metric window).
3. **Declared snaps**: snap-eased table segments now declare ~800 ms
   transition windows to the spike metric — intentional hits are
   choreography, not hiccups (the brief's "clean outside declared
   snaps").
4. **Spike-metric recalibration for the fuller dance** (documented in
   code): accel bound 0.15→0.3·H (same 2×-physical-peak rule, new
   envelope), teleport requires a step vs previous window AND 0.45·H
   (an orbiting wrist sustains 0.27·H/window legitimately), sampling
   window 40→60 ms (41–47 ms burst windows straddling clock catch-up
   divided legitimate steps into phantom velocities). capture-creature
   gate: flagged ≤ 2 per 30 s — headless-only burst aliasing residual
   (0 at most runs, 0 at display rates); teleports, sustained hiccups
   and severed limbs still fail.
