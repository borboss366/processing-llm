## B1/B2 — the channels

render-only) and `yaw` on pelvis/chest (±0.6). Both interpolate like
rot through sampleMove, the rhythm crossfade, the unkeyed spring-back
and the per-channel table springs (a missing spring state crashed the
module on first contact — fixed with lazy per-channel creation).

- cos-bend exact: elbow −0.9 under shoulder twist 1.2 → −0.326 (=cos)
- draw foreshorten exact: humerus 65 px → 24 px (=×cos 1.2), applied to
  the CHILD offset (twist names the bone leaving the joint — the first
  cut scaled the wrong bone)
- dim ≤0.15 per limb, ankle-twist foot-fan widening on the ground tip
  bone, chest-yaw eye parallax
- clamp ±2.0, DEVIATION from the brief's ±1.2: the promised elbow flip
  ("re-emerges bent the other way") requires cos to cross zero at
  π/2 ≈ 1.571 — the brief's own bound forbids its own example.

Extractor: twist channels ride the same cycle averaging; distill emits
them as DEVIATION from the bone's habitual plane (mean-removed — a
constant offset is camera geometry and would permanently foreshorten
the bone; first cut gave the T-step a 0.85 rad standing neck twist).

## B3 — stress row + two found bugs

rotation-stress gains the twist row: cyclic twist sweep ±1.2 on bent
arms + ankle fan ±1.1 + chest yaw ±0.6. PASS: humerus draw length
0.045..0.118 u (range 0.073), max step 0.021 — NO POPPING at the cos
crossings; spikes 0; static scrub clean.

Found on the way:
1. **boneDevRot red herring**: the metric is a running session MAX and
   compared against raw rest length — the twist row's legitimate
   foreshortening (1−cos 1.2 = 64%) poisoned every later assert and
   masqueraded as a stuck pose. Metric now expects the FK's exact
   twist/yaw-scaled length.
2. **Intermittent open issue**: two full-harness runs showed the twist
   channel reading 0 while live and cycling; 7 targeted replications
   (incl. exact-sequence, race-timing, W+cross prefixes) could not
   reproduce. Three permanent tripwires + an end-of-run watchdog keep
   any recurrence loud (harness currently PASSES end-to-end). Note for
   the reviewer: a "twist liveness" check after long move sequences in
   soak runs would localize it if it recurs. A 0.00 reading right after
   a manual section can be phase parking, not death — check the mode.

## C — drop reactivity (grid tier)

- audio.js: one offline decode at file load → per-beat RMS on the
  grid's beat list → bar-aggregated; drop = bar stepping ≥ z 2.2 over
  the trailing 8 bars AND ≥1.25× their mean, ≥8 s apart. Published
  per frame: nextDropMs / nextDropInBeats (null on PLL tier).
  Darude: 4 drops found (15.7, 29.3, 114.6, 149.8 s — 114.6 is THE
  drop).
