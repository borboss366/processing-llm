## Pipeline fix forced by this move: --cycles prior

The unconstrained period search latched a 0.217 s micro-bounce harmonic
on the ~1.5 s roll (ac 0.50) — 27 wrong-period cycles averaged the
whole move away (1 joint survived distill). The subharmonic-descent
bias is safe for step-like moves because decideLoop corrects ×2, but
nothing can climb a ×6.9 error. Fix: `--cycles N` (the logged cycle
count for the window — cheap ground truth the sourcing log now carries)
constrains the search to ±43% of window/N and owns the octave. With
--cycles 4: period 1.486 s, 2/3 cycles kept, 13-joint table. Self-test
still 13/13.
