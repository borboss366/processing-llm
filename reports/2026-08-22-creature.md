# Creature module — first captures (Brief 4, Task 2)

Module: `web/app/loaded-modules/creature.js`. Captured with the file-audio
path (techno mix @ 120 s, Butterchurn background off for legibility) via
`tools/capture-creature.mjs`. 20 s clips + stills, one per shape:

- `creature-quadruped.png` / `creature-quadruped.mp4`
- `creature-jelly.png` / `creature-jelly.mp4`

## What it looks like

**Quadruped** (`shape=quadruped`, trot gait): a wireframe four-legged animal
in side view — teal body mass, magenta head held up-right, four legs in
distinct hues stepping in diagonal pairs on the beat, head bobbing at 2×.
The silhouette reads sheep/dog-like. Weakest part: the feet — the free
tissue below the knee crumples into loose "puddles" around the pinned tips,
so the lower legs read scattered rather than solid.

**Jelly** (`shape=jelly`, pulse gait): a round teal bell with five hanging
beaded tentacles in different hues, bell scale-pulsing on the beat, tentacle
tips swaying together. Clearly a jellyfish; the tentacles fragment into
bead-clusters where the k-nearest graph is sparse, and tips occasionally
flick sideways.

## Performance

| shape | nodes | edges | module physics+draw | page fps (headless swiftshader) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| quadruped | 444 | 791 | 2.37 ms/frame | 24.0 |
| jelly | 442 | 788 | 2.32 ms/frame | 24.4 |

The 60 fps criterion is judged on the module's own cost: 2.4 ms/frame
against a 16.7 ms budget — comfortable, with ~7× headroom before the module
becomes the bottleneck. The 24 fps page rate is the software-GL compositor
ceiling of headless Chrome (the same page idles near 24 fps with the module
disabled); on a real GPU the render window runs at display rate.

## Mechanics verified in the clips

- Gait phase runs off `beatPhase` (confidence-gated, free-run fallback at
  `lastConfidentBpm`) — motion stays on the techno mix's beat and does not
  stop through low-confidence stretches.
- `triggerable`+`fadeable` lifecycle: enters over ~0.9 s when triggered.
- OSC-rebuild works: `/creature/shape` swaps quadruped→jelly live.

## Open question (STOP)

Does it read as a creature? Per the brief: if no, the first fixes are the
shape (clearer limbs, less body mass) and the armature (more pins, stiffer
springs) — not the tissue. The crumpled quadruped feet suggest "more pins
along each limb (mid-leg, not just hip+tip)" as the first armature candidate
if the verdict is mixed.
