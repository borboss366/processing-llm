# Brief 17.6 Task 1 — the `weight` signal

## What it is
`weight ∈ [−1, +1]` per frame: −1 all on the left foot, +1 all on the
right, 0 shared; `null` = both feet up (airborne). Rig body frame.
Code: `tools/mocap/lib/weight.mjs` (rule + contacts⇄weight), extractor
(per frame → SG-smoothed → averaged → distilled per key).

## Rule (per frame, from the rig's OWN FK — never image height)
- A foot whose lowest point (ankle or toe) is above the lower foot's by
  more than 0.06·L is OFF the floor (back down below 0.035·L —
  hysteresis) → weight ±1 toward the other foot. L = the shape's leg
  length (0.295–0.305 u).
- Both down → `2·t − 1`, t = where the pelvis sits between the two
  ankles along the view's axis (x: lateral in front, fore/aft in
  profile). Feet closer than 0.05·L → 0. Dead band |w| < 0.15 → 0.
- Both up → null.
- Distill keeps weight like any channel (its swings choose keys) and
  DERIVES contacts from it (|w| ≥ 0.6 → the free foot is unplanted;
  null → none). One source of truth.

## Deviations (stated)
- **Neither foot planted → null (airborne), not "previous".** The brief's
  "previous" cannot round-trip (twist-smoke's all-empty keys); airborne
  is out of scope and keeps today's behaviour; the round-trip is exact.
- **Feet = ankle x + lowest point.** The brief names ankles; the lift
  test needs the foot's lowest point (ankle OR toe) so a flat foot and a
  rest-tilted foot both read "down".
- **Profile foot zero restored (bug found by self-test d).** The capture
  measures heel→toe (flat = horizontal); the rig's foot bone rests
  tilted ~11°. Since the absolute retarget every flat profile foot drew
  toe-up by that tilt. Profile ankles now use "horizontal toward the
  facing = rest" (the same foot-reference mechanism as front view).
- Tool fix: engine-fidelity/engine-report counted clamp hits over the
  whole page session; now only inside the measured window. (The running
  man's 9/11 pre-window foot clamps happen during the switch-in from the
  previous move — the limit lookup follows the incoming table while the
  blend still plays the outgoing one. Harmless; noted.)
