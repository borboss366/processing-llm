# 2026-09-01 — brief 15 Section C: feet as first-class parts

The gate headline ("the ankle is just two joints following the
direction — it needs to look like a proper foot") is closed at the
geometry level.

## C1 — sampler: the foot owns its axis

Sidecar parts gain `foot0`/`foot1` regions. The sampler fits their ring
chains along the FOOT's own principal axis (which is horizontal once
the foot has its own region — the old failure was feet living inside
the leg region, inheriting the shin's vertical axis), labels the nodes
as the parent limb (pins, splats, density probes, part floors all
unchanged), and welds ring 0 to the LEG chain's existing nodes at the
ankle. The result is an L that can pivot instead of "wider bottom rings
on a vertical chain."
