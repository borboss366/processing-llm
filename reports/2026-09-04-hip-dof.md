# Brief 16.1 — hip rotation DOF

First data-discovered anatomy debt (found by the brief-16 extraction):
the rig had no hip rot, so sideways leg swings were faked as knee/ankle
bends. Patch landed mid-flight in 16.

## What changed

1. **DOF**: `hipL/hipR` joints added to biped-1/2/front sidecars (role
   `hip`, parented to pelvis, at 20% down the old pelvis→knee line;
   knees re-parented). The FK chain, pins, bone splats and the rotLimits
   clamp are generic over sidecar joints — no chain code changed.
   `rotLimits.hip: 0.9` (default + sidecars). Gait entry `hip: A=0` so
   the walk stays pixel-identical (fallback would have inherited root's
   0.03 lean). Note the brief said hips "gain rot" — no hip joints
   existed at all; they were ADDED (deviation in wording only).
2. **Stress harness**: rotation-stress gains the leg row — hip ±0.9
   straight-leg sweeps crossing the stance leg, bent-knee compound,
   lunge snap-hold; static scrub + live + snap.
3. **Retarget**: femur decomposition — hip ← MP hip→knee bone; knee =
   relative femur–tibia; ankle relative tibia–foot (unchanged value by
   construction: accumulated shank rotation is identical).
4. **Housekeeping**: tstep-placeholder's 16 knee keys split hip-led
   (hip = 0.6×old, knee = 0.4×old — authored shank world orientation
   preserved exactly, thigh now swings); MODULE_ABI role/chain/rotLimits
   updated; fk-check asserts kick hip-led.

## Re-extraction proof (T-step clip, same windows)

Per-joint theta variance (rad², whole L-lead window):

| joint | before | after | change |
|---|---|---|---|
| hipL | — | 0.0026 | new |
| kneeL | 0.0034 | 0.0096 | +186% |
| ankleL | 0.2226 | 0.2226 | 0% (invariant by construction) |
| hipR | — | 0.0017 | new |
| kneeR | 0.0113 | 0.0060 | −47% |
| ankleR | 0.0733 | 0.0733 | 0% |

Reading, honestly: the FREE (swinging) leg's knee variance drops 47% —
the hip absorbed the swing, per the brief's acceptance. The WEIGHTED
leg's knee variance rises: with the femur decomposed out, the knee now
reports true relative flexion (body swaying over the planted foot) that
absolute-shank measurement previously hid — genuine anatomy, not faking.
Ankle channels are bit-identical (the decomposition preserves
accumulated shank rotation). The effect is modest on THIS clip because
the t-step barely swings the femur; the DOF pays off visibly in the QA
stickman (bent-knee scissor gone, legs read straight — before/after
frames compared at 0.55 s and 1.15 s) and will matter more for
kicks/knee-lift moves (running man).

Known limitation: audience-drawn shapes get no hips from the submit
enrichment — hip-led tables read flatter on them (documented in
MODULE_ABI; enrichment synthesis is out of 16.1 scope).
