## 2. Depth channels (emitted now, consumed in brief 17)

Per frame in poses.json:
- `footYaw` L/R — floor-plane angle of the 3D heel→toe vector AFTER
  de-yaw (`deYaw3` keeps z). Sample mid-window: L 0.97, R 1.28 rad —
  feet strongly toward camera, the depth story quantified.
- `twist` per observed bone — signed out-of-plane angle
  atan2(Δz, planar) of each segment vs the projection plane
  (`boneTwists`). Sample: ankleL twist 0.78 rad while hipL is 0.13 —
  the foot lives off-plane, the thigh mostly in-plane.

## 3. pose_worker input enhancement

Two-pass VIDEO-mode design: full-frame scout pass → UNION bbox over the
window → two temporally-tracked VIDEO streams on that FIXED crop
(upscaled to short side ≥ 512), one horizontally mirrored (TTA),
averaged after flip-back. `--enhance off` keeps the legacy single-pass
path.

Jitter metric: mean SECOND DIFFERENCE of raw landmarks (px) — a plain
frame-to-frame delta mostly measures the dance; second difference
cancels constant-velocity motion. Numbers (L window):

| path | jitter |
|---|---|
| legacy full-frame VIDEO | 3.75 px |
| first attempt: per-frame moving crop, IMAGE mode + TTA | 4.52 px (WORSE — discarded MediaPipe's temporal prior) |
| shipped: fixed union crop, VIDEO mode ×2 + TTA | **3.37 px (−10%)** |

The failed intermediate is kept in the worker's docstring as a warning.

Side effect: cleaner signals moved an autocorrelation near-tie so the
base period landed on 2× (1.131 s). detectPeriod gained subharmonic
descent (bias toward the smaller peak) — safe by construction because
decideLoop's ×2 test corrects a wrong halving but nothing corrected a
wrong doubling. Self-test period cases still pass; L window back to
0.555 s, 3/3 cycles.
