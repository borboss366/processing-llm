# Brief 17.6 revision 2 — contacts ≠ weight, grounding by one rule

## The correction (extractor)
`contacts` = the foot TOUCHES (the lift detector's per-frame verdict,
averaged per bin like the airborne fraction: touching where lifted in
< half the cycles). `weight` = the foot CARRIES (pelvis between the
ankles). Distill emits both; neither is derived from the other.
`contactsFromWeight` deleted; authored tables keep `contacts`, and
`weight` defaults from them only when absent (`weightFromContacts`).
Stitch crossfade takes contacts from the dominant half (discrete).
Self-test (f), replacing the old contacts→weight→contacts round trip
(which tested the conflation): both feet down, pelvis over the right
ankle → weight +1 AND the left foot touching. PASS.

Re-extracted (all captured): keys where a foot TOUCHES while the other
carries ≥ 0.6 — exactly what the old derivation dropped: runningman 7 of
32, tstep 9 of 56, bodyroll 2 of 14, kick 0 of 25.

## Grounding (engine, replaces T2 step 5)
Drawn bones (revision 1's rest-length grounding reverted):
1. the support foot (weight share, smoothstep shared stance) on the floor;
2. after bounce and sink: if a TOUCHING foot would be below the floor,
   the body rises until the lowest touching foot is on it — feet measured
   again after the tilt (it moves the free leg with its hip);
3. printed per frame (`wl.incons`): the other touching foot's hang =
   leg-depth inconsistency; (`wl.liftedUnder`): a lifted foot under the
   floor = capture error. Neither corrected.
The knee re-solve for the pelvis shift stays. No clamp, no invented knee.
**Deviation, measured:** the floor constraint takes contacts as the table
states them, not through a spring. Sprung, a landing foot sat under the
floor while the spring caught up (layer-measured 0.058 L on
tstep-placeholder, 0.011 tstep); unsprung, 0 everywhere and spikes stay 0
(a foot turns touching AT the floor, so the constraint engages without a
pop).
